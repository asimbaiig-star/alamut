doing careful work.

Nº 01 / Principles

Four rules we
don't break.

Every component, every screen, every decision sits inside these four constraints.

Nº 01
Editorial over dashboard.
When in doubt, lay the screen out like a magazine page. Wide white space. One idea per section. Serif for weight, mono for the label.
Nº 02
A creator is a person, not a metric.
Portraits come before numbers. Names come before follower counts. The reach figure is the last thing on the card, not the first.
Nº 03
Color is earned, not decorative.
One accent (terracotta), used sparingly to mark calls-to-action, current state, and results. Everything else is ink on paper.
Nº 04
No filler. Ever.
If a section feels empty, that's a design problem to solve with layout — not by inventing content. One thousand no's for every yes.

Nº 06 / Voice

Write like
a thoughtful friend.

Sentences have subjects. Numbers are written out when they're small. Em-dashes are allowed — sparingly.

Do
"Alamut represents twelve creators."
Human numbers. Clear subject. No hedging.
Don't
"We work with 12+ top-tier creators to drive ROI."
Vague quantifier. Agency cliché. Metric-first framing.
